### CV ladder (out-of-fold Pearson r per scheme)

The headline rigour check is the **generalization gap down the CV ladder**: random k-fold and leave-station-out (LOSO) sit close here because the synthetic satellite→surface mapping is spatially homogeneous, but the strictest **spatiotemporal-blocked** rung (which buffers folds in both space *and* time) drops clearly — that drop is the spatial+temporal autocorrelation a naive split would hide. On real CPCB data the LOSO gap widens too (stations are heterogeneous). `Δr(STB−rand)` is the leakage the random split overstates; it is negative for every pollutant.

## Honest notes / fallbacks used

- Gap-fill used the **idw** (cKDTree IDW) rung of the DINEOF→U-Net→IDW cascade for speed; the heavier DINEOF/U-Net passes are available via `fusion.gapfill.fill_gaps(method='auto')` for the real pipeline.
- Back-trajectories use the **kinematic Lagrangian fallback** on synthetic winds (no HYSPLIT executable / ARL met available offline); this is the documented fallback in `transport.hysplit`, not a stub.
- The fire→HCHO cross-correlation is run **without deseasonalisation** and co-located over the Punjab/Haryana source box: the record is one 60-day season (no multi-year cycle to remove), so a moving-average detrend would distort the short 0–2 day lag we want. The real multi-year pipeline turns `deseasonalize=True` back on.
- Fire-period decomposition ran via **stl** over a single 60-day burning season. Because the synthetic fires are a sustained seasonal ramp (active most days), few bursts rise above the trend — the fire-activity summary is reported alongside the episode count rather than tuning thresholds to manufacture episodes.
- All heavy/optional deps (torch, earthengine-api, cdsapi, pykrige, xesmf, hdbscan) stayed **unused** — the entire chain ran on the light set.
- Numbers are from synthetic data designed to exercise the full pipeline; they validate the *plumbing and method behaviour*, not real-world skill.
